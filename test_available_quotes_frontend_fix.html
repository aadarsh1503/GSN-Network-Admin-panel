🔧 Available Quotes Filtering Fix Test

Testing the fix to prevent showing quotes that are already accepted or have payment proofs completed by other companies

📋 Fix Details

Issue: New companies were seeing quotes that were already accepted or had payment proofs by other companies

Solution: Enhanced filtering in getAvailableQuotes to exclude:

  • ✅ Quotes with accepted responses (existing)
  • ✅ Quotes with payment proofs uploaded (new)
  • ✅ Quotes with verified payments (new)

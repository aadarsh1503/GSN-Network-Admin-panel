🔄 Payment Rejection → Quote Status Update Test

📋 Test Objective

Verify that when a company rejects a payment from the Payment Management page, the quote status is automatically updated to "rejected" and this change is reflected on the My Quotes page.

🎯 Expected Behavior:

  • Payment rejection on http://localhost:5173/company/payment-management
  • Quote status automatically changes from "pending" to rejected
  • Status change appears on http://localhost:5173/company/my-Quotes
  • Admin notification is created
  • Customer receives rejection email

🏢 Payment Management Page

URL: http://localhost:5173/company/payment-management

Test Scenario:

  1. Navigate to Payment Management page
  2. Find a quote with pending payment verification
  3. Click "Review Payment" button
  4. Click "Reject Payment" button
  5. Provide rejection reason
  6. Confirm rejection

🧪 Testing Checklist

Pre-Test Setup:

  • □ Ensure you have a quote with uploaded payment proof
  • □ Quote should be in "pending" status
  • □ Payment should be in "pending" verification status
  • □ Both pages should be accessible

Test Steps:

  1. □ Open Payment Management page
  2. □ Locate quote with pending payment
  3. □ Click "Review Payment" button
  4. □ Verify payment details are displayed correctly
  5. □ Click "Reject Payment" button
  6. □ Enter rejection reason in modal
  7. □ Click "Confirm Rejection"
  8. □ Verify success message appears
  9. □ Navigate to My Quotes page
  10. □ Verify quote status shows "rejected"
  11. □ Verify payment status shows "Payment Rejected"
  12. □ Check admin panel for new notification

Verification Points:

  • □ Quote status badge is red and shows "Rejected"
  • □ Status dropdown shows "rejected" as selected
  • □ Payment status badge shows rejection
  • □ Timestamps are updated
  • □ Admin notification was created
  • □ Customer received rejection email
  • □ Real-time sync works (if applicable)

Edge Cases to Test:

  • □ Reject payment without providing reason
  • □ Cancel rejection and verify no changes
  • □ Reject already rejected payment

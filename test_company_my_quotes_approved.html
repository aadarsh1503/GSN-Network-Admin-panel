🔍 How It Works

Flow:

  1. User uploads payment proof for a quote response
  2. Company verifies payment in Payment Management panel
  3. System updates quote status to "approved"
  4. Quote now appears in company's "My Quotes" section

Query Logic:

WHERE qr.company_id = ? 
AND (
    uqs.status = 'accepted'  -- Traditional accepted quotes
    OR (q.status = 'approved' AND pv.verification_status = 'verified' AND pv.company_id = qr.company_id)  -- Approved via payment
    OR (q.status = 'approved' AND pp.company_id = qr.company_id)  -- Approved with payment proof
)

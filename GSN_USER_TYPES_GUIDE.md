# Gulf Star Network - User Types Guide

## Overview
Gulf Star Network supports three main user types, each with specific roles and capabilities in the freight forwarding and logistics ecosystem.

---

## 1. Regular User (Individual)

### **Role**: Individual Freight Forwarder / Logistics Professional
### **Purpose**: Personal networking and quote management

### **Key Capabilities:**
- ✅ Submit freight forwarding quotes
- ✅ Browse member directory
- ✅ View available quotes from companies
- ✅ Respond to quote requests
- ✅ Basic messaging with companies
- ✅ Personal profile management
- ✅ View invoices and transaction history

### **Limitations:**
- ❌ Cannot create company profiles
- ❌ Limited quote submissions (based on plan)
- ❌ No team management features
- ❌ Basic support only

### **Typical Use Case:**
*"I'm a freelance freight forwarder looking to connect with companies and respond to shipping opportunities."*

---

## 2. Company Member (Business Entity)

### **Role**: Freight Forwarding Company / Logistics Business
### **Purpose**: Business operations and team collaboration

### **Key Capabilities:**
- ✅ Create and manage company profile
- ✅ Add unlimited team members
- ✅ Post multiple quote requests
- ✅ Manage company branches
- ✅ Advanced messaging system
- ✅ Company-wide notifications
- ✅ Bulk quote operations
- ✅ Company invoicing and payments
- ✅ Team member permissions
- ✅ Company directory listing

### **Subscription Required:**
- 💰 Professional Plan: $99/month
- 💰 Enterprise Plan: $199/month

### **Typical Use Case:**
*"We're a logistics company that needs to manage multiple team members, post regular quote requests, and maintain our business presence in the network."*

---

## 3. Business User (Premium Individual)

### **Role**: Senior Logistics Professional / Business Owner
### **Purpose**: Premium networking and advanced business features

### **Key Capabilities:**
- ✅ All regular user features
- ✅ Priority quote placement
- ✅ Advanced analytics dashboard
- ✅ VIP networking access
- ✅ Premium company showcase
- ✅ Market intelligence reports
- ✅ Dedicated account support
- ✅ Exclusive business events access
- ✅ Enhanced profile visibility

### **Premium Features:**
- 🌟 Featured in directory searches
- 🌟 Priority customer support
- 🌟 Advanced reporting tools
- 🌟 Global partnership network access

### **Typical Use Case:**
*"I'm a senior logistics professional or business owner who wants premium features, priority access, and advanced networking opportunities."*

## Registration Flow

### **Regular User:**
1. Sign up with email
2. Complete basic profile
3. Start browsing quotes immediately
4. Upgrade to paid plans for more features

### **Company Member:**
1. Register company account
2. Choose subscription plan
3. Set up company profile
4. Add team members
5. Start posting quotes and managing business

### **Business User:**
1. Apply for business membership
2. Profile verification process
3. Premium onboarding
4. Access to exclusive features
5. Dedicated account manager assignment

---

## Key Differences Summary

- **Regular Users**: Individual professionals seeking opportunities
- **Company Members**: Businesses managing teams and operations
- **Business Users**: Premium individuals with advanced networking needs

Each user type is designed to serve different segments of the freight forwarding and logistics industry, from individual freelancers to large corporations.

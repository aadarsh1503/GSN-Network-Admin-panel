🔍 Test Payment Verification with Email Notifications

This tool tests the enhanced payment verification system with email notifications to customer, company, and admin.

🔑 Authentication

Get this from browser localStorage.getItem('token') after logging in as a company

📋 Payment Verification Details

This should be the payment_verification_id from the database
For rejections, this will be sent to the customer explaining why the payment was rejected

📧 Expected Email Notifications

When payment is VERIFIED:

  • Customer: "✅ Payment Verified - Work Starting Soon"
  • Company: "✅ Payment Verified - Begin Work"
  • Admin: "✅ Payment Verified - Quote #[ID]"

When payment is REJECTED:

  • Customer: "❌ Payment Verification Issue" (includes rejection reason)
  • Company: "❌ Payment Rejected - Quote #[ID]" (confirmation)
  • Admin: "❌ Payment Rejected - Quote #[ID]" (includes payment proof link)

🔧 Setup Instructions

  1. Login as Company: Use the company panel to login
  2. Get Token: Open browser dev tools → Console → Type: localStorage.getItem('token')
  3. Find Verification ID: Check the payment management page or database for payment_verification_id
  4. Test Verification: Choose verify or reject and add appropriate notes
  5. Check Emails: Verify that all parties receive the correct email notifications
